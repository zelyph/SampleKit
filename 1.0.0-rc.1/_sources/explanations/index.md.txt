# Explanations

How SampleKit works, and why: one notion per page, each the same on the
command line, in the TUI, in Python and in the files. Read them in any order;
the [tutorial](../tutorial/index.md) practises them, the
[how-to guides](../how-to/index.md) apply them to a task, and the
[reference](../reference/index.md) gives the exact syntax.

- [Samples and their files](samples-and-files.md): a sample is one Markdown
  file; what its frontmatter and its note hold, and what SampleKit writes.
- [Values, uncertainty and units](values-and-uncertainty.md): a value, its
  uncertainty, its unit and its digits, and why missing is not zero.
- [Readings and statistics](readings-and-statistics.md): repeated
  measurements, and why their value is a statistic you choose.
- [Models and formulas](models-and-formulas.md): your Python formulas, what
  each one reads, and how and where SampleKit runs them.
- [Current and outdated values](current-and-outdated.md): how SampleKit knows
  a computed value is still right, and what each state means.
- [History](history.md): every change kept, taken back, or read as it was,
  and where an exported file came from.
- [Projects](projects.md): the configuration a sample follows, and several
  projects in one view.
